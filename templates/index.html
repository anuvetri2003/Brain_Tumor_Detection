BRAIN TUMOR DETECTION

Brain tumors are abnormal growths of cells in the brain that can affect vital functions such as memory, movement, and speech. Early and accurate detection plays a crucial role in improving patient outcomes and survival rates. Advances in artificial intelligence have enabled faster and more reliable analysis of medical images. This system uses AI-based techniques to identify tumor regions with high precision. By supporting early diagnosis, the model assists doctors in planning timely and effective treatment. Such technology has the potential to enhance clinical decision-making and patient care.
